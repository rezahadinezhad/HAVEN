## Evidence presentation

Prefer a short behavior/evidence table over a trophy wall of numbers. Strong examples are interrupted encrypted sends, persisted-vault reload, revoked authority, encrypted media, unanimous account closure, restore-deny enforcement, and hostile API code rejection while a vault is unlocked.

The existing 166-test backend milestone, 30 focused final closure checks, 13 guided-installer checks, and 14 verified-client checks are different suites with different scopes. Some overlap. Do not combine them into a headline total. The six CI jobs are checks of the recorded source, not six independent audits.

## Reading older records

ADRs and initiative reports preserve the state at their dates. Some describe an earlier blocked/default-disabled phase or the earlier same-origin-only client. Explain the present implementation using current code/status and link those records as history or scoped evidence. Do not erase old failure notes or imply that a newly added control was present in older test runs.
