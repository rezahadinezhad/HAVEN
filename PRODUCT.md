# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A small invite-only trusted community using English or Persian on phones and
desktops, evaluating private, self-hosted communication and explicit device trust.

## Product Purpose

Private browser messaging without a purchased domain, foreign runtime services
or another user-managed device. Minimize retained server data while preserving
verified identities, durable delivery and clear security limitations.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, approachable. The owner's iOS-inspired direction is captured in
DESIGN.md and the existing interface; preserve that system.

## Anti-references

The former oversized beige interface, fake conversations, decorative security
badges, implementation-heavy onboarding and claims of guaranteed anonymity.

## Design Principles

- Make the next real action clear.
- Explain consequences before irreversible operations.
- Show observed connection and identity state truthfully.
- Keep sensitive state on the device and disclose remaining server metadata.
- Preserve familiar chat interactions without extra infrastructure for users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English/Persian, complete RTL and mixed-direction content, keyboard focus,
readable contrast, touch targets, reduced motion and responsive small screens,
as required by the master specification and established design system.
